Privacy Policy

Mind1Train — Mind Training

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Policy version: 1.1

Developer: Vladimir Minchenko
Contact: mind1train.apps@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Mind1Train — Mind Training processes information, how data is stored and deleted, and how to contact the developer. It applies to the Android, Windows and PWA versions to the extent that their features are implemented.

1. App name and developer information

App: Mind1Train — Mind Training; short name: Mind1Train.

Developer: Vladimir Minchenko. Using the trainer does not require registration or an account.

2. What data the app processes

The app does not request your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising identifiers, and it does not create user accounts.

Only the data needed for the trainer is stored on the device:

  • the selected interface language;
  • the selected arithmetic operation, time limit, difficulty, sound and animation settings;
  • counts of correct answers, mistakes, and timeouts;
  • the all-time record: difficulty, operation, actual solve time, both operands, the correct answer, date and time.

3. Local storage of records, statistics and settings

All of this information is stored locally on the device: in browser localStorage for the PWA, in the Windows user profile at %APPDATA%\Mind1Train, or in the Android app's internal storage.

Local keys use the mind1train_ prefix: language, settings, record, statistics, and data format version.

Local storage is still processing of data, even when nothing is transmitted over a network. Data is not synchronized between devices and is not available to the developer.

4. Internet use

An internet connection is not required to solve arithmetic exercises.

When connected, the app may check for updates through Google Play. Installing updates requires internet access and depends on device and app-store settings.

Opening external pages or contacting the developer by email may involve processing by those services. The PWA downloads its files from its hosting website when first opened or updated; the host may maintain technical access logs.

5. Sharing data with third parties

Exercise results, records, and settings are stored locally and are not sent to the developer.

If you voluntarily email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are processed to handle and reply to your request. Email providers process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

System services, Google Play, and web hosts may independently process technical information under their own policies.

6. Advertising and analytics

The version covered by this policy does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

If ads or analytics are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before release to identify the services, categories of data, purposes and third-party disclosures. Google Play Data Safety information and any required consent mechanisms will also be updated.

7. Data retention

Local data remains until you reset it in Settings, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or clear the PWA site data.

There is no automatic deletion schedule. Records and statistics may remain indefinitely until you delete them. The developer keeps no server-side copy.

8. Deleting user data

In the app, open Settings and use “Reset record” or “Reset statistics”. Language, sound and animation settings can be changed in Settings.

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Mind1Train → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local data.
  • Windows: delete the %APPDATA%\Mind1Train folder, or remove the app folder together with its user data.
  • PWA: clear site data in the browser settings, or uninstall the PWA.

If you email the developer, the information in your message is processed to respond; the developer cannot delete local results on your device.

9. Data security

Local data lives in the app or browser sandbox. The app and the operating system can access it; there is no network transmission.

The app does not use its own storage encryption. Protection depends on device encryption, screen lock, and operating system security policies. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

The Windows build uses context isolation, sandboxing, disabled Node.js, CSP, and the local app:// scheme. The Android build uses the Capacitor WebView and the app sandbox. The PWA is isolated by the browser.

10. Use by children

Mind1Train is a mental arithmetic trainer for a broad audience, including teenagers and adults. Children who have learned basic arithmetic may also use the app.

The app does not require registration or a user account. Exercise results and settings remain on the device. Applicable requirements for apps accessible to children are considered when publishing updates.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. A new version is published with a new effective date and is available in the app and at the public address.

Material changes may be announced in the app or app store. Continued use after publication means you have reviewed the updated policy.

12. Developer contact

Developer: Vladimir Minchenko. For privacy questions, feedback, suggestions or requests, email mind1train.apps@gmail.com.

When contacting the developer, users voluntarily provide the information included in their email. The developer cannot remotely access locally stored records or settings.

13. Effective date

Effective November 14, 2026 (version 1.1).
